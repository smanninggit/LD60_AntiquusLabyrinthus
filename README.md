
You find yourself trapped in an old labyrinth of brick walls and stone floors. Stuck with only an old-school torch to light your way, try to escape before it goes out. Keep a look out for ancient traps that still work, and cultists who want to stick you in some medieval torture devices. Fight back with your sword, and keep an eye on your limited bolts for the crossbow. 

When the torch timer goes out, you lose. When your health runs out, you lose. Get to the final room and open the big door if you want to escape. Oh and by the way, we're timing you, so don't think you can walk on through with seconds left and get a gold. 

WASD/ZQSD = movement  
Shift to sprint  
E = interact  
1,2 or mouse wheel = change weapons  
Left click = attack with the sword or shoot the crossbow  
Right click = stab with the sword or reload the crossbow  
Tab/Enter = Menu  
  
Made in Unity and Blender, programming and music by Trusty, art and voice acting by Cheeks. No AI used.
